---
name: Brand site content audit
description: Protects factual business websites from invented details introduced during visual implementation.
---

For factual brand sites, compare the finished copy and calls-to-action against the approved business brief after the visual build. Keep phone, address, ratings, hours, pricing, testimonials, and business history centralized and never invent missing facts.

**Why:** Visual implementation can accidentally add plausible but unsupported claims or replace exact business details with generic content.

**How to apply:** Run a final grep/content audit before presenting the site, then verify every phone link, address, rating, review count, and editable placeholder against the source brief.
